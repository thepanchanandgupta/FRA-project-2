Methodology & data integrity

How the data was cleaned, and what is and is not verified

Data layers

Raw CSVs are kept untouched ( files, SHA-256 identical to the uploads). A cleaned layer flags every row. Event-study outputs are calculated separately.

Calendar

regular trading days from 3 Oct 2016 to 30 Sep 2026. Muhurat sessions are preserved but excluded from the primary calendar.

Window & adjustments

Research window fixed at 1 Oct 2016–30 Sep 2026. Stocks use dividend- and split-adjusted prices; indices are price-return, a documented mismatch.

Exclusion log

Coverage & event windows

Event-study method

  • Daily return = adjusted closet / adjusted closet−1 − 1, on consecutive regular trading days only. If either day is missing the return is blank (never filled).
  • Market model Ri = α + βRm + ε estimated on trading days [−250, −31] before the reaction day (at most 220 returns, at least 120 required).
  • Days inside ±20 trading days of any other event are excluded from the estimation sample (this removes the COVID crash from the HDFC Dec-2020 estimation).
  • AR = actual − (α + βRm); CAR = ΣAR over the window; t = CAR / (σε√L). Windows: [−1,+1], [−3,+3], [−5,+5], [−10,+10], [−20,+20].
  • Where a window contains a missing index day it is flagged and left incomplete; the next available day is never substituted.
  • Abnormal returns are associated with, not proven to be caused by, credit news: earnings, rates and other news can move the same days.
