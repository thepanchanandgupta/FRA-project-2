01 / Executive view

What the data says

Two different lending models. Separate peer groups. One shared question: how much of credit risk is institution-specific?

01

Different asset-quality profiles

HDFC Bank’s GNPA stayed near 1.1–1.4% over the extracted decade; Bajaj Finance’s rose to 1.79% in FY2021 before moderating to 1.01% in FY2026.

02

Peer selection matters

Bank advances and NBFC AUM are not interchangeable. Peer comparisons therefore stay within the banking and NBFC sectors.

03

Write-offs can hide deterioration

Gross NPA additions, recoveries, write-offs and provisioning must be read together. A lower closing GNPA alone does not tell the full story.

07 / Data treatment

Data breaks & how we handled them

Structural and reporting changes considered when interpreting the ten-year series.

HDFC Ltd merger · effective 1 July 2023

FY2024 onward includes the merged mortgage book. We retain the reported series and flag FY2024 as a structural break; balance-sheet growth is not treated as wholly organic.

ICICI Bank NNPA denominator

The historical NNPA series changes denominator basis. We retain source observations and identify the break; movements across the boundary are not treated as fully like-for-like.

Cholamandalam accounting basis

FY2017 uses IGAAP and later years use Ind AS. Stage 3 measures are kept distinct from NPA measures.

COVID-19 regulatory relief

Moratorium and standstill measures delayed NPA recognition. FY2021 ratios are read in the context of this timing effect.

Sector denominators

Banks report advances; NBFCs commonly report AUM or business assets. Peer analysis is therefore kept within sector groups.

Market data basis

Equity series use adjusted close; index benchmarks use index close. The different return bases are retained rather than blended.
