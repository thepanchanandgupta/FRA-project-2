01 / Executive view

What the data says

Two different lending models. Separate peer groups. One shared question: how much of credit risk is institution-specific?

01

Different asset-quality profiles

HDFC Bank’s GNPA stayed near 1.1–1.4% over the extracted decade; Bajaj Finance’s rose to 1.79% in FY2021 before moderating to 1.01% in FY2026.

02

Peer selection matters

Bank advances and NBFC AUM are not interchangeable. Peer comparisons therefore stay within the banking and NBFC sectors.

03

Write-offs can hide deterioration

Gross NPA additions, recoveries, write-offs and provisioning must be read together. A lower closing GNPA alone does not tell the full story.

04 / Flow-based credit risk · priority analysis

Slippages & write-offs

Why this matters

A lender may report a lower GNPA ratio after recoveries, upgrades or write-offs. Slippages and write-offs reveal the movement behind the headline ratio.

New NPA additionsWrite-offs
InstitutionGross NPA additionsGross NPA reductions / recoveriesWrite-offsSlippage ratioWrite-off ratioCredit cost
Comparability note. Absolute amounts are in ₹ crore and depend on loan-book size and reporting perimeter. Supplementary academic values are distinguished from reported figures. Slippage ratio = gross additions ÷ average advances/AUM; write-off ratio = write-offs ÷ average advances/AUM; credit cost = impairment/provision charge ÷ average advances/AUM. These are academic ratios where company-reported denominators are unavailable.
BANKS

HDFC Bank & ICICI Bank

Both disclose gross NPA additions and write-offs. ICICI FY2026 reported gross additions of ₹19,147 crore and write-offs of ₹8,424 crore. HDFC’s figures are transcribed from the FY2026 annual-report extraction and need final source-page verification.

SYSTEMATIC RISK · COMMON EXTERNAL SHOCKS

Sector-wide pressure on funding, borrowers and collections

Transmission: macroeconomic or policy shocks affect multiple lenders at once through liquidity, funding costs, employment, borrower cash flows and repayment capacity.

  • COVID-19: lockdowns impaired repayment capacity; moratorium and standstill measures delayed NPA recognition.
  • Russia–Ukraine shock: commodity inflation and risk-off sentiment affected costs and market valuations.

Expected footprint: correlated stress or valuation pressure across several lenders, although exposure varies by business mix.

IDIOSYNCRATIC RISK · ENTITY-SPECIFIC EVENTS

Institution-level governance, operations and portfolio decisions

Transmission: a lender-specific event can affect its own asset quality, operating permissions, reputation or market valuation more than its peers.

  • ICICI Bank: Videocon-related governance scrutiny and leadership transition occurred during its corporate-NPA resolution cycle.
  • HDFC Bank: RBI restrictions on digital launches highlighted technology and operational-control risk; the HDFC Ltd merger is treated separately as a reporting-perimeter break.
  • Bajaj Finance: RBI restrictions on eCOM/Inst EMI Card lending exposed product-level conduct and control risk.

Expected footprint: a more pronounced response in the affected institution than in its sector peers.

Risk classification is analytical, not a claim of sole causation. Event-window returns are descriptive; they do not prove that an event caused a credit-quality change.

06 / Method & evidence

Methodology, breaks & sources

Enough context to reproduce the analysis—without crowding the main dashboard.

!
Academic data disclosure

All ten companies have FY2017–FY2026 GNPA, NNPA and PCR observations. Where source observations were unavailable, supplementary academic values are included for analysis and are not company-reported figures.

A

Scope

HDFC Bank (large private-sector bank) and Bajaj Finance (lending NBFC). Each is evaluated against four listed peers in its own sector.

B

Market data

Daily history from 3 Oct 2016 to 30 Sep 2026. Equity adjusted close; Nifty Bank and Nifty Financial Services sector indices; Nifty 50 as a broad reference.

HDFC Ltd merger · effective 1 Jul 2023

FY2024 onward includes the merged mortgage book. Balance-sheet scale, advances and credit mix are not directly comparable with pre-merger years.

ICICI NPA denominator

Early NNPA observations use a customer-asset basis in the extracted pack; later observations use net advances. Historical comparisons must show the break.

Chola reporting basis

FY2017 is IGAAP; subsequent figures use Ind AS. GNPA/NNPA and Stage 3 ratios are distinct measures.

COVID moratorium / standstill

Regulatory relief delayed NPA classification. FY2021 reported asset-quality ratios should not be read as a clean measure of underlying stress.

Sector denominators

Banks report advances; NBFCs often report AUM/business assets. Capital ratios and PCR definitions also vary by institution.

07 / Data treatment

Data breaks & how we handled them

Structural and reporting changes considered when interpreting the ten-year series.

HDFC Ltd merger · effective 1 July 2023

FY2024 onward includes the merged mortgage book. We retain the reported series and flag FY2024 as a structural break; balance-sheet growth is not treated as wholly organic.

ICICI Bank NNPA denominator

The historical NNPA series changes denominator basis. We retain source observations and identify the break; movements across the boundary are not treated as fully like-for-like.

Cholamandalam accounting basis

FY2017 uses IGAAP and later years use Ind AS. Stage 3 measures are kept distinct from NPA measures.

COVID-19 regulatory relief

Moratorium and standstill measures delayed NPA recognition. FY2021 ratios are read in the context of this timing effect.

Sector denominators

Banks report advances; NBFCs commonly report AUM or business assets. Peer analysis is therefore kept within sector groups.

Market data basis

Equity series use adjusted close; index benchmarks use index close. The different return bases are retained rather than blended.
